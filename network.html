Livonne Network

Livonne Network helps turn a need the system already understands into real-world support. It begins with family and people the household trusts. When they cannot cover it, the circle can widen to known local helpers, approved providers, and formal services.

The person remains in control. Livonne shares only what the task requires, prepares the person helping, and brings the outcome back into the household so the next request does not start from zero.

How help widens

Livonne does not begin with a marketplace. It begins with the relationships already surrounding the person, then adds outside help only where it is useful.

Family and friends

The people already inside the support relationship can receive the request, the relevant context, and a clear way to respond without taking on the entire coordination burden.

Known local helpers

A trusted neighbor might deliver a meal, handle the lawn, provide a ride, or help with an ordinary task. Livonne makes the need clear without exposing the rest of the household’s life.

Approved providers

When the work requires a professional, Livonne can share the information authorized for that appointment or service—such as access instructions, timing, and the reason for the visit—without opening unrelated household memory.

Formal services

When family and local relationships cannot cover a need, Livonne can help reach a broader service. The formal option is available without becoming the first answer to every problem.

Livonne Network in practice

Ron’s support does not come from one person or one company.

His daughter remains part of the decisions that matter. Sally brings meals on the days she is available. A neighbor takes over the lawn. A physical therapist arrives with the context approved for the visit. When the local circle cannot cover dinner, a formal service fills the gap.

No one receives Ron’s entire history simply because they are helping with one task.

Livonne Network helps the right person understand what is needed, what they are allowed to know, and what happens next.
