Ambient AI

Help should not depend on the perfect prompt.

Most AI waits for someone to open an application, understand what it can do, and ask the right question.

Livonne lives closer to everyday life. With permission, it can recognize who is present, understand the room, notice routines, bring forward useful information, and respond when something needs attention—even when no one thinks to ask.

That is ambient AI: intelligence available in the moment instead of hidden behind a prompt box.

That requires a lot of sensing.

A system that can understand daily life must be designed with stronger boundaries than an ordinary smart-home product.

That is why Livonne is built around sovereign AI.

A home that notices should also know when to forget.

Traditional monitoring systems often send household signals somewhere else: a cloud service, a remote monitoring center, or a screen watched by people the household may never meet.

Livonne works differently.

Household signals are interpreted locally first. The system keeps the useful understanding and discards raw information that has no reason to survive. When outside help is needed, Livonne shares the minimum necessary information with the person or service authorized to receive it.

Your home does not become a feed. It becomes more capable.

We keep only what helps.

Raw household information is disposable by default. Livonne preserves useful context, not a permanent recording of daily life.

You own what remains.

Your memories, routines, relationships, permissions, and personal history belong to you. They should be portable, correctable, and removable.

The outside world gets only what it needs.

Livonne can stand between the household and the public internet, reducing unnecessary exposure and revealing personal information only when the task legitimately requires it.
