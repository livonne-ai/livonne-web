How product claims are handled

Livonne is a concept-stage system. Public descriptions are grounded in the current product, privacy, and narrative design. Working prices, component assumptions, roadmap order, and proposed capabilities are not evidence of validation or availability.

This site intentionally avoids testimonials, clinical claims, retail promises, and performance claims that have not been supported by approved evidence.
