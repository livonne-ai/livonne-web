Livonne Community

Livonne Community gives residents more than a place to live. It creates a connected neighborhood where people can find activities, share meals, make friends, reach family, get where they need to go, and ask for help without turning every need into a formal care event.

Livonne quietly coordinates the place around them—transportation, dining, activities, maintenance, shared resources, and appropriate support. Residents gain more freedom to move through community life, while staff spend less time watching dashboards and managing routine handoffs and more time being present when people actually need them.

Key devices

1 / 4

Device 1 of 4

Livonne Surface AIO

The resident’s private Livonne experience inside their apartment. Surface AIO carries their familiar assistant, routines, preferences, permissions, family relationships, and selected memories into their new home without making them start over.

Device 2 of 4

Community Hub

The shared intelligence that coordinates the place. Community Hub connects dining, transportation, activities, maintenance, staff workflows, common resources, and community-wide services while keeping each resident’s private continuity separate.

Device image in development

Device 3 of 4

Care Band

A simple wearable that connects the resident to the community. It can provide identity, access, personalized shared experiences, communication, and an easy way to ask for help without feeling like a hospital bracelet.

Device 4 of 4

Shared Community Surfaces

Indoor and outdoor surfaces provide directions, activities, transportation updates, shared events, and community connection. They adapt when a resident approaches, then return to a general community view when that person leaves.

The Community story

The Community

Ron has spent years protecting his independence at home. Livonne has helped him stay there longer than his family thought possible.

But the practical needs are no longer the real problem.

Ron is lonely.

He agrees to one tour of a Livonne Community. He expects to inspect the apartment, find the catch, and go home. Instead, he finds his own kitchen, his own assistant, coffee with someone willing to argue about football, a chess table by the pond, and a community that already knows how to help without taking over.

Ron arrives determined not to surrender the life he fought to keep.

He begins to wonder whether that life might become larger again.
